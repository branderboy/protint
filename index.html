Quick Answers

TINTING QUESTIONS

More in the full tint FAQ.

How much does window tinting cost at Pro Tint DMV?

Cars are a flat $108 for every window. Pickups are $60 for the two front door windows. Full-size SUVs, vans, extra truck windows and ceramic film upgrades are quoted by phone at (301) 307-1414 because glass count and size vary so much.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking online is the best way to lock in a bay, especially on Fridays and Saturdays. Walk-ins are welcome when a technician is free, but an appointment guarantees your slot.

How long will my car be in the shop?

Plan on 45 minutes for two front windows and up to two hours for a full car. Most customers wait in the lobby or grab lunch nearby.

What tint percentage is legal in Maryland?

Maryland requires at least 35% visible light transmission on every window of a passenger car, including the rear. We install 35% as our standard legal shade and will show you the options before we cut any film.

Is the lifetime warranty real?

Yes. Our film carries a lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, fading and turning purple. If a panel ever fails, bring the vehicle to any of our four shops and we replace it at no charge.
