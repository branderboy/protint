About the Upper Marlboro Shop

WHAT MAKES UPPER MARLBORO DIFFERENT

Upper Marlboro is the county seat and the Route 301 and Route 4 interchange funnels traffic from every direction, so this shop sees a bit of everything: courthouse and county employees on lunch-hour appointments, farm and landscaping trucks from the Croom side, and Marlton and Westphalia families with newer SUVs. The bays here handle larger vehicles comfortably, so if you have a full-size pickup or an extended SUV and want every window done, this is a good shop to call.

Maryland tint law at this shop

Every car we tint in Upper Marlboro is a Maryland-registered car unless you tell us otherwise, so we install 35% on all windows for passenger cars and 35% on the front doors of trucks and SUVs. Drivers coming from DC should mention it when booking so we stock the right shade. Full limits are in our Maryland and DC tint law guide.

Warranty service here

Any Pro Tint install, from any of our four shops, can be checked and serviced at Upper Marlboro. Bring the vehicle in during business hours or book a warranty visit and note it. Terms are on the warranty page.
