About the Bowie Shop

WHAT MAKES BOWIE DIFFERENT

Bowie is our busiest suburban location. The Route 301 corridor is full of commuters heading to DC, Annapolis, and Fort Meade, and a lot of them spend an hour or more a day behind the wheel in direct sun. That is exactly the driver who feels the difference heat-rejecting film makes. We also tint a lot of family SUVs and minivans here for parents who want the back seats cooler and the car seats out of view.

Maryland tint law at this shop

Every car we tint in Bowie is a Maryland-registered car unless you tell us otherwise, so we install 35% on all windows for passenger cars and 35% on the front doors of trucks and SUVs. Full limits are in our Maryland tint law guide.

Warranty service here

Any Pro Tint install, from any of our four shops, can be checked and serviced at Bowie. Bring the vehicle in during business hours or book a warranty visit and note it. Terms are on the warranty page.

Getting Here

LANDMARKS AND DIRECTIONS

Reference points around the Bowie shop and how to reach us from them.

Bowie Town Center

The shopping center at Route 301 and Route 50. Our shop is on Crain Highway (Route 301) a few minutes from the interchange, so most customers pass Town Center on the way in.

Allen Pond Park

Bowie's main park off Northview Drive. From Allen Pond, Route 197 or Mitchellville Road brings you out to Route 301.

Belair Mansion

The historic house at the center of the Belair section. Belair customers reach us by way of Route 197 or Route 450 to Route 301.

Bowie Railroad Museum (Old Bowie)

At the north end of the city on Route 564. Old Bowie customers take Route 564 or Route 197 south to Route 301.

Two ways in

Coming from Bowie Town Center? Get onto Crain Highway (Route 301) and look for 4406, Suite B. We are a few minutes from the Route 50 interchange.

Coming from Crofton or Odenton? Take Route 3 south, which becomes Route 301 at the county line, and continue to 4406 Crain Hwy.

Bowie FAQ

LOCAL QUESTIONS

Which Pro Tint location should I use if I live in Crofton or Odenton?

Bowie. Route 3 runs straight into Route 301 and brings you to the shop. Upper Marlboro is the alternative if you are already heading south on 301.

Where can I get window tint near Bowie Town Center?

Our Bowie shop at 4406 Crain Hwy, Suite B, is on the same Route 301 corridor as Town Center. Book online and plan on 45 minutes for a truck or up to two hours for a full car.

Is Bowie the right shop for a three-row SUV?

Yes. Bowie tints a lot of family SUVs and minivans from the Belair and Mitchellville side. Call first so we can quote the window count and reserve enough bay time.

Do you take walk-ins in Bowie?

When a technician is free, yes. Weekday mornings are the best bet. Booked appointments always go first.
