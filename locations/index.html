Service Area

WHERE OUR CUSTOMERS DRIVE IN FROM

Between the four shops we cover the whole of Prince George's County and a good stretch beyond it. Capitol Heights pulls from Southeast and Northeast DC, Largo and District Heights. Bowie serves the Route 50 and Route 301 corridor up into Anne Arundel County: Crofton, Odenton and Gambrills. Clinton covers Joint Base Andrews, Fort Washington, Oxon Hill and the Route 5 commuters from Waldorf and Brandywine. Upper Marlboro is the stop for Marlton, Croom, Westphalia and anyone coming across Route 4.

If you are not sure which shop is closest, call and we will tell you. Hours differ slightly: Capitol Heights and Clinton open at 8am, Bowie and Upper Marlboro at 9am. All four are closed Sunday.

DC drivers

We do not have a shop in the District, but Capitol Heights is minutes from the Southern Avenue and East Capitol Street crossings. DC-registered cars have different tint limits than Maryland cars, so tell us when you book. The numbers are in our Maryland and DC tint law guide.
