About the Clinton Shop

WHAT MAKES CLINTON DIFFERENT

Clinton serves a wide stretch of southern Prince George's County and northern Charles County. A big share of our Clinton customers are military families and base personnel from Joint Base Andrews, along with Route 5 commuters who want a cooler cabin for the drive up to the Beltway. Pickups and work trucks are common here, which is why the two-front-window truck package is our most requested service at this shop.

Maryland tint law at this shop

Every car we tint in Clinton is a Maryland-registered car unless you tell us otherwise, so we install 35% on all windows for passenger cars and 35% on the front doors of trucks and SUVs. Full limits are in our Maryland tint law guide.

Warranty service here

Any Pro Tint install, from any of our four shops, can be checked and serviced at Clinton. Bring the vehicle in during business hours or book a warranty visit and note it. Terms are on the warranty page.

Getting Here

LANDMARKS AND DIRECTIONS

Reference points around the Clinton shop and how to reach us from them.

Surratt House Museum

The historic tavern on Brandywine Road in the center of Clinton. From the museum, Brandywine Road to Old Branch Avenue brings you to Coventry Way in a few minutes.

Joint Base Andrews

The main gate is up Allentown Road from Branch Avenue. Base personnel make up a large share of our Clinton customers.

Branch Avenue Metro station (Green Line)

At the top of Branch Avenue toward the Beltway. Customers from Temple Hills and Camp Springs come down Route 5 from there.

Branch Avenue (Route 5) and Old Branch Avenue

The two roads that bracket the shop. Coventry Way runs between them.

Two ways in

Coming from the Surratt House Museum? Take Brandywine Road to Old Branch Avenue, then turn onto Coventry Way. We are at 6311, Suite B.

Coming from Waldorf or Brandywine? Head north on Route 5 (Branch Avenue), exit toward Old Branch Avenue in Clinton, and look for Coventry Way.

Not sure you are in the right lot? Call (301) 307-1414 and we will talk you in. Open the shop on Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions.

Clinton FAQ

LOCAL QUESTIONS

Which Pro Tint location should I use if I live in Fort Washington or Oxon Hill?

Clinton. It is the closest of our four shops to the Indian Head Highway side of the county, usually fifteen to twenty minutes by way of Route 210 and Route 5 or Allentown Road.

Where can I get window tint near Joint Base Andrews?

Our Clinton shop at 6311 Coventry Way, Suite B, is a few minutes from the base down Branch Avenue. We tint a lot of trucks and sedans for base personnel and can usually fit a two-window truck job into a lunch hour.

Do you serve Waldorf and Charles County?

Yes. Waldorf, Brandywine and Accokeek drivers use the Clinton shop. We do not have a shop in Charles County.

Do you take walk-ins in Clinton?

Yes, when a bay is open. Truck front-window jobs are quick, so walk-ins for those are the easiest to fit in. Book online for a full car.
