About the Clinton Shop

WHAT MAKES CLINTON DIFFERENT

Clinton serves a wide stretch of southern Prince George's County and northern Charles County. A big share of our Clinton customers are military families and base personnel from Joint Base Andrews, along with Route 5 commuters who want a cooler cabin for the drive up to the Beltway. Pickups and work trucks are common here, which is why the two-front-window truck package is our most requested service at this shop.

Maryland tint law at this shop

Every car we tint in Clinton is a Maryland-registered car unless you tell us otherwise, so we install 35% on all windows for passenger cars and 35% on the front doors of trucks and SUVs. Drivers coming from DC should mention it when booking so we stock the right shade. Full limits are in our Maryland and DC tint law guide.

Warranty service here

Any Pro Tint install, from any of our four shops, can be checked and serviced at Clinton. Bring the vehicle in during business hours or book a warranty visit and note it. Terms are on the warranty page.
