ABOUT THESE JOBS

Are these your own installs?

Yes. Every photo was taken in a Pro Tint bay during or right after an install.

Can I get the same shade as a car in the gallery?

If the car is Maryland-registered, the shade on a passenger car is 35% on all windows, which is what most of these are. Trucks and SUVs can go darker behind the driver. Bring the photo to your appointment and we will match it where the law allows.

Do you tint equipment and work vehicles?

Yes. The cab glass on the excavator in the gallery is a good example. Call with the machine or vehicle and we will quote it.

Which shop did each job?

All four shops install the same film to the same standard, so the result looks the same at any location. Book at the shop closest to you.
