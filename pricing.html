Pricing FAQ

PRICING QUESTIONS

Is the $108 car price really all windows?

Yes. Front doors, rear doors or quarter glass, and the rear windshield, on any sedan, coupe, hatchback or wagon. There is no per-window math and no upcharge for a two-door.

Why is a truck cheaper than a car?

The $60 truck package is two windows, not five. Most pickups have factory privacy glass in the back, so the two front doors are all that needs film. If you want the rear glass done too, call and we quote it.

What does an SUV cost?

It depends on how many pieces of glass and whether the rear is already factory tinted. A crossover with clear glass all around is a different job from a Suburban with privacy glass. Call with the year, make and model and we quote it in a minute.

How much more is ceramic?

It scales with glass area, so we quote it per vehicle. Expect meaningfully more than carbon because the film costs more; the labor is the same.

Is there a deposit or booking fee?

No. Book online, show up, pay when the work is done. Cash and cards accepted.

Do you price match?

We would rather explain the difference. A cheaper quote almost always means dyed film with a short warranty or no warranty. Ask any shop what happens when their film turns purple.
