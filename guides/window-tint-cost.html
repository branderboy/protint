What Moves the Number

SIX THINGS THAT CHANGE A TINT QUOTE

1. How many windows

This is the biggest factor. A coupe has four pieces of glass plus the rear. A three-row SUV can have nine. More glass means more film and more labor.

2. How big and curved the glass is

A flat door window is a five-minute install once it is cut. A steeply curved rear windshield on a coupe, or the big quarter glass on a Tahoe, has to be heat-shrunk on the outside of the glass to take its shape before it can be installed on the inside as one piece. That is skilled, slow work and it is what you are paying a professional for.

3. Film type

Standard dyed-carbon film is included in our posted prices. Ceramic film costs more per foot because it blocks far more heat. The install labor is the same; the film is what changes the price.

4. Factory privacy glass

If your truck or SUV already has dark glass in the back, you only need the fronts, which is a $60 job instead of a full-vehicle quote. Always tell us whether the rear is already tinted.

5. Old film that has to come off

Removal is quoted separately. Film that is merely too dark peels off in sheets. Film that has baked for a decade comes off in flakes and can take an hour per window, plus adhesive cleanup.

6. Shade and legality

Shade itself does not change the price. We stock 35% as the Maryland standard and lighter shades for DC-registered cars. Going darker than the law allows is not something we offer on Maryland cars, so it does not enter the quote.

Cheap vs. Done Right

WHY $108 IS CHEAPER THAN $70 TWICE

You will see tint advertised around the DMV for less. Here is what usually makes it cheaper and what it costs you later.

  • Film with unstabilized dye that turns purple in two to three summers
  • No warranty, or a warranty from a shop that may not be there next year
  • Rear windows done in two or three strips instead of one shrunk piece
  • Film trimmed on the glass with a razor, which scores the glass and nicks the seals
  • No interior glass prep, so dust and hair end up under the film

When that tint fails, you pay for removal and then pay for new film. Our price includes the right film and a lifetime warranty, so the second bill never comes. That is the whole argument. Pick whichever shop you like, but ask every one of them what happens when the film turns purple.
