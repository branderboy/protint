The Terms, Plainly

WHAT THE WARRANTY ACTUALLY SAYS

Window film has two ways to fail. The film itself can break down, which shows up as fading, a purple cast or a cloudy look. Or the installation can fail, which shows up as bubbles that never cleared, edges that lift or a corner that starts to peel. Our warranty covers both for as long as you own the vehicle.

Covered

  • Bubbles or blisters that remain after the two-week cure period
  • Peeling or lifting at any edge or corner
  • Fading, discoloration or a purple tint to the film
  • Cracking, crazing or delamination of the film layers
  • Adhesive failure of any kind

Not covered

  • Glass that cracks, chips or breaks for any reason
  • Scratches from razor blades, abrasive pads, seat belts, cargo or pets
  • Damage from ammonia or solvent cleaners
  • Film reworked, removed or reinstalled by another shop
  • Vehicles that have changed ownership since the install

How a claim works

Call (301) 307-1414 or book a warranty visit online and mention it in the notes. A technician looks at the panel with you, confirms it is a film or install failure and schedules the replacement. Most single-panel replacements are done the same day. There is no charge and no paperwork beyond confirming you are the original owner.

Why we can afford to offer this

Because we almost never have to use it. Clean glass, the right film and patient installers mean the failure rate is very low. Most of the warranty calls we get turn out to be cure haze in the first week, which clears on its own. Read our aftercare guide if you are unsure what is normal and what is not.

Warranty FAQ

WARRANTY QUESTIONS

What does the Pro Tint lifetime warranty cover?

Bubbling, peeling, lifting at the edges, fading, discoloration and the film turning purple. If the film or the installation fails in any of those ways, we remove the affected panel and install new film at no charge.

How long does the warranty last?

For as long as you own the vehicle the film was installed on. It does not transfer to a new owner when the vehicle is sold.

What is not covered?

Glass that cracks or breaks, film damaged by razor blades, abrasive pads, ammonia cleaners or items rubbing against the inside of the glass, and film removed or reworked by another shop. Normal cure haze and small water pockets during the first week are not defects.

Do I need to bring paperwork?

Bring your invoice if you have it. If you do not, we can look you up by name and plate at any of our four shops, since every Pro Tint install is logged in the same booking system.

Can I use the warranty at a different Pro Tint shop than where I had the install?

Yes. Capitol Heights, Bowie, Clinton and Upper Marlboro all honor each other's work.
