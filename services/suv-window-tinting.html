How We Price It

WHAT CHANGES THE PRICE ON AN SUV

Three things drive the quote: how many pieces of glass you have, how big and curved they are, and whether the rear already has factory privacy tint.

Window count

A compact crossover like a RAV4, CR-V or Rogue typically has two front doors, two rear doors and a rear hatch window. A three-row SUV like a Tahoe, Expedition or Pilot adds two large rear quarter windows, and some have a split rear hatch glass. Minivans have sliding door glass and big fixed quarter panels. Cargo vans may have no rear glass at all. We quote per piece so the number matches your vehicle.

Factory privacy glass

If your SUV came with dark glass behind the front doors, you probably only need the two front windows tinted to match. That job is priced like the truck front-window package at $60. If the rear glass is clear, we quote the full vehicle.

Panel size and curve

Large curved quarter glass on a three-row SUV takes real time to heat-shrink and fit as one piece. That is where a lot of shops leave seams or trim the film short. We budget the bay time to do it right, and the quote reflects that.

Typical appointment lengths

  • Front two windows only: about 45 minutes
  • Two-row crossover, all windows: about 90 minutes
  • Three-row SUV or minivan, all windows: 2 to 3 hours
  • Cargo van with rear glass: quoted on the phone

Who Books SUV Tint

FAMILIES, COMMUTERS AND FLEETS

Parents

Car seats in direct sun get hot enough to burn. Tinted rear glass keeps the second and third rows cooler and keeps the kids and their gear out of view in a parking lot.

Commuters

The front doors on a crossover are big pieces of glass right next to your face. Tinting them to 35% takes the edge off the afternoon sun on Route 50 and Route 301.

Work Vans and Fleets

Tools and inventory in a van are a theft target. Dark rear glass means nobody is window shopping. Call about multi-vehicle scheduling at one shop.

SUV Tint FAQ

SUV & VAN QUESTIONS

Why is there no posted price for SUVs?

Because SUV glass varies more than any other vehicle class. A two-row crossover has five windows. A three-row SUV can have nine, with big curved quarter panels. We count your windows on the phone and quote the real number so you are not paying a sedan price for a Suburban or a Suburban price for a RAV4.

What information do you need for a quote?

Year, make, model and whether it already has factory privacy glass in the rear. With that we can quote it in about a minute at (301) 307-1414.

Does my SUV already have tint?

Most mid-size and full-size SUVs and all minivans come with dark privacy glass behind the front doors. Compact crossovers often do not. If yours does, you may only need the two front windows, which is priced like the truck package at $60.

Can the rear windows go darker than 35% on an SUV?

Yes. Maryland classes SUVs and vans as multipurpose vehicles, so only the front door windows have to meet 35%. Windows behind the driver can be as dark as you like.

How long does a full SUV take?

Two to three hours for a full three-row SUV with no factory tint. Front windows only is about 45 minutes.
