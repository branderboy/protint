Ceramic Window Tint

CERAMIC FILM: SAME LEGAL SHADE, A LOT LESS HEAT

Ceramic window film uses non-metallic ceramic particles to block infrared heat that ordinary dyed film lets through. You get a noticeably cooler cabin at the same 35% shade, with no signal interference. Quoted per vehicle.

The Difference

WHAT CERAMIC DOES THAT DYED FILM DOES NOT

Sunlight carries heat mostly in the infrared band, which you cannot see. A standard dyed film mainly controls visible light. It darkens the glass, blocks UV and cuts glare, and it does reduce some heat, but much of the infrared still comes through. Ceramic film adds a layer of ceramic nanoparticles that absorb and reflect infrared specifically. The result is a window that looks exactly as dark as 35% dyed film but lets far less heat into the car.

Who notices the difference

  • Drivers with black or dark leather interiors that get painfully hot
  • Anyone who parks outside all day at work
  • Long-distance commuters who want the AC to keep up on the Beltway in July
  • EV and hybrid owners who want to run the AC less and keep range
  • Customers who tried cheap tint before and felt no heat difference

No metal, no interference

Some high-performance films of the past used metal layers to reflect heat. They worked, but they could weaken cell reception, GPS and toll transponders, and they had a mirrored look. Ceramic film gets its heat rejection without metal, so there is no signal problem and no mirror effect. It looks like a clean, neutral charcoal tint.

Same warranty, same install

Ceramic film is installed exactly like our standard film and carries the same lifetime warranty. The only differences are the film itself and the price. Because the cost scales with glass area, we quote it by vehicle rather than posting one number.

Ceramic Tint FAQ

CERAMIC QUESTIONS

Is ceramic tint darker than regular tint?

No. Shade and heat rejection are separate things. Ceramic film is available at the same 35% legal shade as our standard film. It blocks more infrared heat at that shade, not more light.

Will ceramic tint interfere with my phone or toll transponder?

No. Ceramic film has no metal in it, so it does not affect cell signal, GPS, satellite radio or E-ZPass. Older metallized films could; ceramic does not.

How much more does ceramic cost?

It depends on the vehicle, so we quote it by phone. Expect it to run meaningfully more than the standard package because the film itself costs more. If you drive a lot in summer or have a black interior, most customers feel it is worth it.

Does ceramic have the same warranty?

Yes. Lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, fading and discoloration, same as our standard film.

Can I do ceramic on just the front windows?

Yes. A common setup is ceramic on the two front doors, where the driver feels the heat most, and standard film on the rear. We can price it either way.
