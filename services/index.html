How Pricing Works

WHY TWO FLAT PRICES AND A PHONE NUMBER

Most tint shops publish a price range and then quote something higher once the car is in the bay. We would rather post the real number. A car is $108 because a sedan, coupe or hatchback has a predictable set of glass: two front doors, two rear doors or quarter windows, and a rear windshield. A truck is $60 because most pickups only need the two front windows tinted to match the factory privacy glass behind them.

SUVs and vans break that pattern. A compact crossover might have five pieces of glass. A three-row SUV can have nine, with large curved quarter panels that take real time to shrink and fit. Rather than publish a number that is wrong for half of them, we count the windows with you on the phone and give you a firm quote before you book.
