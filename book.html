Before Your Appointment

  • Plan on 45 minutes for two front windows, up to 2 hours for a full car
  • Remove anything stuck to the inside of the glass: toll transponders, dash cams, suction mounts, stickers
  • If old tint needs to come off first, tell us when you book so we reserve extra time
  • Rain is fine. Film goes on the inside of the glass, so weather does not affect the install
